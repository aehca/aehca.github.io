Impact in Ipala

See what our community partners and supporters accomplished in 2026, and our continued commitment for 2027.

View the full-size infographic

Read the infographic as text

In 2026, AEHCA and its partners reported the following work in Ipala:

  • 190 senior adults and people with disabilities received basic food needs.
  • 15 post-secondary students received scholarships.
  • 20 patients received needed medications or tests.
  • 8 families’ homes were stabilized with new walls or foundations.
  • 300 children across 12 schools received newly refurbished desks.
  • 26 students gained access to a school library for the first time.
  • 30 families received electricity through solar panel installations.

Plans for 2027 include expanding support for medications and tests, homes and desks; creating two new libraries; and helping Ipala work toward electricity for every home.
